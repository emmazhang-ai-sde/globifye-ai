06 · On the call path

Generation

What turns retrieved evidence into the actual words a caller hears — retrieval never gets read aloud directly.

The choice

Constrained free generation — the model writes from a prompt of the standing playbook plus this turn's evidence. No evidence, no generation at all — a fixed line takes over.

Why

Evidence is never in a form fit to be spoken; and a model asked to answer with no evidence is exactly when it's most likely to fabricate — so it's never put in that position.

The landscape

How much freedom to give the model

A

Fixed template

No model call at all. Zero latency, zero hallucination risk — and zero flexibility.

B

Extractive

Quote or lightly rephrase the evidence. Locked to the source, but reads stiffly.

CChosen

Constrained free

Writes freely, required by the prompt to answer only from supplied evidence.

D

Generate-then-verify

A second pass checks the output against sources. An extra net, at double the latency.

Where GlobiFYE landed

The one open item

Streaming is tied to a separate TTS-streaming timeline, not a generation decision made in isolation — worth checking that timeline directly rather than trusting a stale note here.

When a model has clear evidence and is told to answer only from it, it tends to comply. Under pressure to answer with no evidence, that compliance measurably drops — a pattern common across generative models, not one model's flaw. Removing the opportunity entirely is the more reliable fix, which is why "don't generate" beats "trust the model not to guess."
