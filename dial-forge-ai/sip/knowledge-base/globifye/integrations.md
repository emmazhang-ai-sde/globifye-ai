# DialForge — Integrations

DialForge connects to the systems a business already runs, so calls turn into data in the right place.

## Telephony
- SIP trunking to bring your existing phone numbers and carrier.
- Direct number provisioning if you want a new line for the agent.
- Works alongside your current phone system rather than replacing it.

## CRM
- Salesforce and HubSpot: log calls, create and update contacts, attach transcripts and outcomes.
- Generic CRM sync via API for other systems.

## Calendars
- Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 for booking, rescheduling, and availability checks.

## Automation
- Webhooks fire on call events (started, ended, analyzed) so you can push data anywhere.
- Zapier / Make connectors for no-code workflows.

## Knowledge base sources
The agent is grounded in your content. Sources can include:
- Markdown, PDF, and Word documents.
- FAQ and help-center exports.
- A structured product or pricing sheet.

## API
A REST API exposes calls, transcripts, analyses, and contacts for teams that want to build their own reporting or workflows.

## Availability
Standard integrations (SIP, CRM logging, calendar, webhooks) are included from the Growth plan. Custom or on-premises integrations are scoped as part of Enterprise onboarding.
