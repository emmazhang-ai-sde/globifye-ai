# DialForge — Product Overview

DialForge is an AI voice agent that handles real phone calls for a business, inbound and outbound, over its existing phone numbers.

## How a call works
1. A call connects over SIP to a DialForge agent (the customer keeps their own number).
2. Speech-to-text transcribes the caller in real time.
3. A language model composes a reply, grounded strictly in the customer's knowledge base.
4. Text-to-speech speaks the reply back in a natural voice, typically within about a second.
5. If the agent cannot answer or the caller asks for a person, it hands off to a human or captures a callback.
6. After the call, DialForge produces a transcript and a post-call analysis.

## Grounded, not generic
DialForge answers from the customer's own knowledge base — products, pricing, policies, hours — rather than from open-ended model knowledge. When a question is not covered, it says so and captures the caller's name and number instead of guessing.

## What it is good at
- Answering common questions accurately and consistently, 24/7.
- Qualifying and routing inbound leads.
- Making outbound follow-up and reminder calls.
- Capturing contact details and booking follow-ups.

## What it is not
DialForge is not a full contact-center platform and does not replace complex human negotiations. It handles the high-volume, well-scoped calls so people can spend time on the ones that need them.
