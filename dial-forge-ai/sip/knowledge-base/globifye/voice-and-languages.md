# DialForge — Voice and Language

## Voices
DialForge offers a range of natural TTS voices in different tones and genders. Each agent is assigned a voice that fits the brand, and voice is configurable per agent. Custom voices are available as an add-on for customers who want a distinct brand sound.

## Latency
The platform targets a natural conversational pace — typically around one second from the end of the caller's speech to the start of the reply. Barge-in is supported, so a caller can interrupt and the agent stops speaking and listens, the way a person would.

## Languages
English is fully supported. Additional languages are available for supported voices; the agent detects and responds in the caller's language where enabled. Multilingual deployments are configured per agent so each line answers in the right language.

## Natural conversation
- Handles interruptions and back-channel ("uh-huh", "right") without losing the thread.
- Confirms names, numbers, and spellings back to the caller to avoid errors.
- Keeps replies short and spoken, since they are heard, not read.

## Quality and accuracy
Speech recognition is tuned for phone-quality audio. When the agent is unsure of what it heard — a name or a phone number — it asks the caller to repeat or confirm rather than guessing, and it answers only from the knowledge base.

## Accessibility
Because the agent is voice-first and available 24/7, callers reach a consistent, patient responder at any hour, including outside business hours when a human is not available.
