# Pacific Beef Trading — Grades and Quality Programs

## USDA quality grades
We supply the three export-relevant USDA quality grades, determined by marbling and maturity:
- **Prime** — abundant marbling, limited supply, premium chilled markets.
- **Choice** — the workhorse export grade; consistent marbling at scale.
- **Select** — leaner, price-sensitive markets and further processing.

Buyers may also specify "upper two-thirds Choice" for programs that want above-average marbling without full Prime pricing.

## Marbling and Angus-type programs
Our premium marbling program selects for higher intramuscular fat and a consistent grain, comparable to branded Angus specifications. It is available on ribeye, striploin, and tenderloin for chilled export.

## Grass-fed and grain-fed
- **Grain-fed** — conventional corn-finished cattle; higher marbling, our default supply.
- **Grass-fed** — pasture-raised, leaner profile; available on standing programs with lead time, with source verification for markets that require it.

## Aging
Chilled export product is wet-aged in vacuum packaging in transit and storage. Buyers can specify a minimum days-aged target on arrival for premium loin cuts.

## Consistency
Grade is verified at the packing establishment and documented per lot. Because we aggregate across plants, we hold to a written grade tolerance on every order so a buyer's program stays consistent shipment to shipment.
