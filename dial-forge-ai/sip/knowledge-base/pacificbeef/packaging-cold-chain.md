# Pacific Beef Trading — Packaging and Cold Chain

## Primary packaging
- **Chilled** — individual cuts vacuum-packaged (Cryovac) to protect shelf life and support wet-aging in transit.
- **Frozen** — vacuum-packaged and blast-frozen; some processing cuts are combo-bin or carton packed.

## Cartons and labeling
Cuts are packed in wax-lined or poly-lined export cartons, typically 20 to 27.5 kg net, labeled with product name, cut code, grade, plant establishment number, production and use-by dates, net weight, and country of origin. Buyer-specific labeling and language are available on standing programs.

## Temperature
- Chilled product ships at -1.5 to 0 °C.
- Frozen product ships at -18 °C or colder.

## Ocean freight
Product moves in temperature-controlled reefer containers, roughly 25 metric tons per 40-foot high-cube. We book carriers, set and monitor container temperature, and can provide temperature logs on arrival.

## Shelf life
Chilled vacuum-packed loin cuts carry a typical shelf life of 90 to 120 days from pack date under continuous refrigeration; frozen product, 12 to 18 months. Actual shelf life depends on cut, cold-chain integrity, and destination requirements.

## Incoterms
We quote FOB U.S. port or CIF destination port. Under CIF we arrange ocean freight and marine insurance; the buyer clears customs and handles inland delivery.
