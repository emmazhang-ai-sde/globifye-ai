# Pacific Beef Trading — Pricing and Terms

## Pricing basis
Beef is a traded commodity, so prices move weekly with the U.S. cutout and cattle markets. We quote against current market and hold a quote for a stated validity window (typically 3 to 5 business days). Quotes are per pound or per metric ton.

## Incoterms
- **FOB** U.S. port of loading — buyer arranges ocean freight and insurance.
- **CIF** destination port — we arrange freight and marine insurance to the named port.

## Minimum order
Standard minimum is one 40-foot reefer container (roughly 25 metric tons), which may be a single cut or a mixed-cut load. Mixed loads carry a small handling premium.

## Payment terms
- Irrevocable letter of credit (L/C) at sight against shipping documents, or
- Telegraphic transfer (T/T): a deposit on order confirmation with balance against copy documents before release.
Terms for new buyers are typically L/C or advance until a trading history is established.

## What a quote includes
Cut and grade, weight range, chill/freeze state, packaging, price and Incoterm, quote validity, estimated production and shipment lead time, and the documents provided.

## Currency and adjustments
Quotes are in U.S. dollars. Freight and fuel surcharges under CIF are passed through at booked rates. Firm pricing is confirmed on the proforma invoice at order.
