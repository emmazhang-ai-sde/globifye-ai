# GlobiFYE — Company Overview

GlobiFYE is a software company that builds **DialForge**, an AI voice agent platform for businesses. DialForge answers and places phone calls in a natural spoken voice, grounded in each customer's own knowledge base, so companies can handle more calls without adding headcount.

## Mission
Make every business phone call answered, accurate, and fast. Most missed revenue on the phone is not a bad conversation, it is a call nobody picked up. DialForge closes that gap.

## What we build
A voice agent that connects to a business's existing phone numbers over SIP, listens and speaks in real time, answers only from the customer's knowledge base, and hands off to a human when it should. Every call produces a live transcript and a post-call analysis.

## By the numbers
- Founded: 2023
- Product: DialForge (AI voice agents)
- Deployment: cloud, connected to the customer's telephony over SIP
- Customers: sales teams, support desks, clinics, and service businesses

## Who we are
A small, technical team spanning speech, language models, and telephony. We focus on one thing, phone calls, rather than a broad contact-center suite, which is why DialForge is fast to deploy and grounded rather than generic.

## How buyers usually start
Most customers begin with a single agent on one use case, inbound sales or support, run a two-week pilot against real calls, then expand to more agents and outbound once the results are clear.
