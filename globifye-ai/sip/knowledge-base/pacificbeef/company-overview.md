# Pacific Beef Trading — Company Overview

Pacific Beef Trading is a U.S.-based exporter of chilled and frozen beef, founded in 2004 and headquartered in Omaha, Nebraska. We source from USDA-inspected packing plants across the Midwest and Great Plains and ship to importers, distributors, and food-service buyers in more than 20 countries.

## What we do
We are an export trading company, not a packer. We aggregate supply across multiple federally inspected establishments, match it to each buyer's cut, grade, and packaging specification, and manage export documentation and cold-chain logistics to the destination port.

## By the numbers
- Founded: 2004
- Headquarters: Omaha, Nebraska, USA
- Export volume: roughly 40 million pounds per year
- Markets served: 20+ countries across Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, and the Americas
- Formats: chilled (vacuum-packed) and frozen

## Programs
We run several sourcing programs to match buyer needs: conventional grain-fed, grass-fed, premium Angus-type marbling programs, and Halal-certified production for eligible markets. All product is USDA FSIS inspected and export-eligible for the destination country.

## How we work
Most relationships start with a small trial container so a buyer can validate grade consistency and cold-chain performance before committing to standing monthly volume. From there we build a recurring supply plan with agreed cuts, grades, pricing basis, and delivery cadence.
