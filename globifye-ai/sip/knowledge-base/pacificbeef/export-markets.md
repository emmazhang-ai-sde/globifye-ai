# Pacific Beef Trading — Export Markets

We ship to more than 20 countries. Demand differs sharply by market, so we tailor cut mix, grade, and format to each destination.

## East Asia
- **Japan** — highest-value chilled market. Strong demand for chilled striploin, ribeye, and short plate; buyers are grade- and marbling-sensitive.
- **South Korea** — large volume in short plate, chuck roll, and short ribs for barbecue; both chilled and frozen.
- **China / Hong Kong** — broad demand across cuts, brisket, and variety meats; eligibility rules change periodically and we ship only against current eligibility.
- **Taiwan** — chilled loin and short plate; quality-focused.

## Middle East
Halal-certified product for the Gulf states, with demand for forequarter cuts, offal, and frozen product suited to long transit.

## Americas
- **Mexico** — high volume in rounds, trimmings, and variety meats.
- **Central and South America** — frozen forequarter and processing cuts.

## Market access
Each market has its own eligibility, labeling, and certificate requirements administered through USDA FSIS export programs. We confirm current eligibility for the specific plant, cut, and destination before quoting, and we never ship product that is not export-eligible for the buyer's country.
