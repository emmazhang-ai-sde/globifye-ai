# Belle Beauty Co. — Knowledge Base

## About us
Belle Beauty Co. is a full-service beauty salon and day spa. We help clients look and feel their best with hair, skin, nail, and makeup services. Our tone is warm, welcoming, and unhurried — clients should feel pampered from the first hello.

## Location and hours
- Address: 128 Rosewood Avenue, Suite 5.
- Tuesday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Sunday and Monday: closed.

## Services and starting prices
- Women's haircut and style: from $55.
- Men's haircut: from $35.
- Full color: from $95. Balayage or highlights: from $140.
- Blowout: from $45.
- Classic facial (60 min): from $85. Deep-cleansing facial: from $110.
- Manicure: from $30. Gel manicure: from $45.
- Pedicure: from $50. Spa pedicure: from $65.

- Special-occasion makeup: from $75.

Prices are starting points; the final price depends on hair length, condition, and the stylist. Give ranges, not exact quotes, and offer to confirm at booking.

## Booking and cancellation
- Appointments are recommended; walk-ins are taken when a chair is free.
- To book, we take the client's name, the service they want, and a preferred day and time, then confirm by text.
- Please give 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Same-day cancellations may be charged 50% of the service.
- New clients coloring their hair need a quick complimentary consultation first.

## Payment and policies
- We accept all major cards and cash. Gratuity is appreciated and not included.
- Gift cards are available in any amount.
- We use cruelty-free products and offer fragrance-free options on request.

## When you don't know
If a caller asks about something not covered here — a specific stylist's availability, a service we don't list, or a medical skin condition — tell them a team member will follow up, and offer to take their name and number. Never invent prices, availability, or medical advice.
