# Maison Boutique — Knowledge Base

## About us
Maison Boutique is an independent women's fashion boutique. We carry a curated selection of contemporary apparel, accessories, and a small line of fine jewelry, and we offer one-on-one personal styling. Our tone is chic, attentive, and confident without being pushy.

## Location and hours
- Address: 40 Marchmont Street, ground floor.
- Monday to Saturday: 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
- Sunday: 12:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
- Online store is open 24/7 at the boutique's website.

## What we carry
- Women's apparel: dresses, knitwear, blouses, tailored trousers, denim, outerwear.
- Accessories: scarves, belts, handbags, and a rotating selection of costume and fine jewelry.
- Sizes run from XS to XL (US 0 to 16). Select styles are available in extended sizes online.
- Price points: tops typically $60 to $150, dresses $120 to $320, outerwear $200 to $600. Give ranges, not exact prices, unless the caller names a specific item.

## Personal styling
- Complimentary 45-minute styling appointments, in store or by video call.
- Great for a specific event, a seasonal refresh, or building a capsule wardrobe.
- To book, take the client's name, the occasion or goal, and a preferred day and time.

## Orders, shipping, and returns
- Online orders over $150 ship free; otherwise standard shipping is $8, arriving in 3 to 5 business days.
- In-store pickup is available, usually ready within two hours.
- Returns and exchanges accepted within 30 days with the tag attached and receipt. Final-sale items cannot be returned.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Payment and policies
- We accept all major cards, and buy-now-pay-later at checkout.
- Gift cards are available in store and online.
- Alterations on purchased garments start at $20 and take about a week.

## Current collection and promotions
- The new season "Atelier" collection has just landed: soft tailoring in warm neutrals.
- Members of our mailing list get early access to sales and 10% off their first online order.

## When you don't know
If a caller asks about stock of a specific size or item, an order's live status, or anything not covered here, tell them a team member will follow up, and offer to take their name and number. Never invent stock levels, order status, or prices.
