# DialForge — Frequently Asked Questions

**Will it sound like a robot?**
No. DialForge uses natural voices and responds in about a second, with barge-in so callers can interrupt. Most callers have a normal conversation.

**How does it avoid making things up?**
The agent answers only from your knowledge base. When a question is not covered, it says so and captures the caller's name and number instead of guessing.

**What happens when it can't handle a call?**
It hands off to a human if one is available, or captures a callback with the context already gathered. It fails safe rather than continuing blindly.

**Do we have to replace our phone system?**
No. DialForge connects to your existing numbers over SIP and runs alongside your current setup. You can also provision a new number.

**How long does setup take?**
About a week for a single use case. The two-week pilot doubles as onboarding.

**Is it secure?**
Yes — SOC 2 Type II, encryption in transit and at rest, configurable retention and redaction, and a HIPAA configuration under a BAA for healthcare.

**How much does it cost?**
Plans start at $500/month (Starter) and $2,000/month (Growth), plus per-minute usage beyond the plan. Enterprise is custom. Final pricing depends on agents and call volume.

**Can it make outbound calls too?**
Yes — inbound and outbound, including follow-ups, reminders, and re-engagement.

**Which languages does it support?**
English fully, with additional languages available for supported voices and configured per agent.
