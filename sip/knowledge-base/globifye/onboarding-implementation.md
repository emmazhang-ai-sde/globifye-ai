# DialForge — Onboarding and Implementation

Most customers are live within about a week. There is no hardware and no rip-and-replace of the phone system.

## Steps
1. **Kickoff** — confirm the use case (inbound sales, support, booking), the number to use, and the success criteria.
2. **Knowledge base** — you provide your content (documents, FAQ export, pricing sheet); we load it so the agent answers from it.
3. **Agent setup** — configure objective, tone, persona, voice, and the fallback and handoff behavior.
4. **Telephony** — connect your existing number over SIP, or provision a new one.
5. **Testing** — run test calls, review transcripts, and tune answers and handoff rules.
6. **Go-live** — point real traffic at the agent, starting with a slice if you prefer.

## Timeline
- Standard single-use-case go-live: about one week.
- Enterprise with custom integrations: scoped during kickoff.

## What we need from you
- The phone number and telephony details (or a request to provision one).
- Your knowledge content.
- One point of contact for testing and sign-off.

## Ongoing
- The knowledge base can be updated any time; changes take effect on the next refresh.
- The dashboard shows live calls, history, and analyses.
- Support by email on all plans; priority and dedicated support on Growth and Enterprise.

## Pilot to production
The two-week pilot doubles as onboarding: by the end you have a tuned agent, real transcripts, and a usage estimate to size the production plan.
