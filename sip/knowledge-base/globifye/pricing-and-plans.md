# DialForge — Pricing and Plans

Pricing combines a monthly plan (agents and included minutes) with per-minute usage beyond the plan. Annual billing saves versus monthly.

## Plans
- **Starter — from $500 / month.** 1 agent, up to 500 included minutes, live transcript and post-call analysis, email support. For a single use case or a pilot.
- **Growth — from $2,000 / month.** Up to 5 agents, 3,000 included minutes, CRM and calendar integrations, webhooks, and priority support.
- **Enterprise — custom.** Unlimited agents, custom integrations, SSO, role-based access, dedicated support, and SLAs.

## Usage
Minutes beyond the plan are billed per minute at a rate that steps down with volume. A typical inbound call runs two to four minutes. We provide a usage estimate during the pilot so there are no surprises.

## What is included
All plans include grounding in your knowledge base, the fallback-and-capture behavior, live transcript, and post-call analysis. Integrations and team features scale up by plan.

## Add-ons
- Additional agent seats.
- Custom voice.
- HIPAA configuration (see security and compliance).

## Pilot
New customers typically run a two-week pilot on the Starter plan against real calls before committing to Growth or Enterprise. Give ranges when discussing price; final pricing depends on agents and expected call volume and is confirmed in the proposal.
