# DialForge — Use Cases

DialForge is deployed per use case: one agent, one job, one knowledge base. Common deployments:

## Inbound sales and lead qualification
Answer every inbound call, qualify the caller against your criteria, book a meeting or route hot leads to a rep, and capture details for the rest. Nothing goes to voicemail.

## Customer support (tier 1)
Handle the repetitive questions — order status, hours, policies, how-to — grounded in your help content, and escalate anything complex to a human with the context already captured.

## Appointment booking and reminders
Book, reschedule, and confirm appointments against a connected calendar; make outbound reminder calls to cut no-shows.

## Reception and call routing
Act as a front-desk receptionist: greet callers, answer common questions, and route or transfer to the right person or department.

## Outbound follow-up
Run outbound campaigns for follow-ups, renewals, and re-engagement, logging outcomes and handing warm responses to the team.

## Who uses it
- Sales teams that miss inbound calls or want faster lead response.
- Support desks with high repetitive-question volume.
- Clinics and service businesses that live on the phone for bookings.
- Operations teams paying for after-hours or overflow coverage.

## Starting point
Most customers pick the single use case with the clearest cost — usually missed inbound calls or after-hours coverage — prove it in a pilot, then expand.
