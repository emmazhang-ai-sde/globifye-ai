# Pacific Beef Trading — Certifications and Compliance

All product originates in federally inspected establishments and ships with the documentation each destination requires.

## Federal inspection
Every plant we source from operates under USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS) inspection and a HACCP food-safety plan. Product carries the USDA mark of inspection and the establishment number.

## Export certification
We provide the FSIS export certificate of wholesomeness and any country-specific health certificate required for the destination, issued against the plant's current eligibility for that market.

## Halal
For eligible markets we supply Halal-certified beef produced under approved Islamic slaughter and certified by a recognized certifying body, with the Halal certificate accompanying each shipment.

## Additional programs
On request and with lead time we can provide:
- Source and age verification (birth-to-harvest) for markets that require it.
- Never-Ever (no added hormones / no antibiotics) program documentation.
- Certificate of origin and non-GMO / non-BSE attestations where required.

## Traceability
Product is traceable by lot back to the harvest establishment and production date. See the food-safety and traceability document for lot handling, testing, and recall procedures.
