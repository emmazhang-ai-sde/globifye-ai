# Pacific Beef Trading — Frequently Asked Questions

**Are you a packer or a trader?**
A trading company. We source across multiple USDA-inspected establishments and match supply to each buyer's specification, which lets us offer a wider cut and grade range than any single plant.

**What is your minimum order?**
One 40-foot reefer container, roughly 25 metric tons, as a single cut or a mixed-cut load.

**Can you supply Halal beef?**
Yes, for eligible markets, produced under approved Islamic slaughter and certified by a recognized body, with the Halal certificate on each shipment.

**Chilled or frozen?**
Both. Chilled (vacuum-packed) suits high-value loin programs with strong cold-chain; frozen suits longer transit and processing markets.

**How is pricing set?**
Against the current U.S. market, quoted per pound or per metric ton, held for a stated validity window (usually 3 to 5 business days).

**What payment terms do you offer?**
Letter of credit at sight or telegraphic transfer. New buyers typically start on L/C or advance until a trading history is established.

**Do you handle shipping?**
We quote FOB or CIF. Under CIF we arrange ocean freight and marine insurance to the destination port; the buyer clears customs.

**Can we start small?**
Yes. Most buyers begin with a single trial container to validate grade and cold-chain before moving to standing monthly volume.

**Which markets are you eligible for?**
Eligibility is plant-, cut-, and country-specific and changes over time. We confirm current eligibility before quoting and ship only export-eligible product.
