# Pacific Beef Trading — Food Safety and Traceability

## Food-safety system
All source establishments operate under a HACCP plan and USDA FSIS inspection, with sanitation standard operating procedures (SSOPs) and validated interventions during harvest and fabrication.

## Pathogen testing
Product is subject to the source plant's testing program for pathogens including E. coli O157:H7 (and, for applicable products, non-O157 STEC) and Salmonella. Certificates of analysis are available on request for programs that require them.

## Lot control and traceability
Every carton is coded to a production lot traceable to the harvest establishment and date. Given a lot code, we can trace a shipment back to its source plant and production day, which supports both quality investigations and any regulatory recall.

## Cold-chain monitoring
Container temperature is set at booking and monitored in transit. Temperature logs (and, where fitted, data-logger records) can be provided on arrival to demonstrate cold-chain integrity.

## Recall and withdrawal
If a food-safety issue arises, we can identify affected lots by code and notify the buyers who received them, coordinating with the source establishment and FSIS as required. Buyers are asked to retain lot codes through to their own customers to preserve end-to-end traceability.

## Documentation
Standard shipments include the export health certificate, packing list, and lot detail. Additional records — certificates of analysis, temperature logs, source verification — are available by prior arrangement.
