# Pacific Beef Trading — Ordering Process and Lead Times

## Steps
1. **Inquiry** — the buyer shares destination market, cuts and grades, monthly volume, and format (chilled or frozen).
2. **Eligibility check** — we confirm the cut and plant are export-eligible for the destination country.
3. **Quote** — we issue a quote (cut, grade, weight range, packaging, price, Incoterm, validity).
4. **Proforma and order** — on acceptance we issue a proforma invoice; the buyer confirms with a purchase order and opens the L/C or sends the deposit.
5. **Production** — product is cut, graded, and packed to spec at the source establishment.
6. **Documentation** — export health certificate, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and any market-specific documents are prepared.
7. **Shipment** — product is loaded in a monitored reefer container and sailed to the destination port.

## Lead times
- Frozen product from inventory: typically 1 to 2 weeks to loading.
- Chilled or made-to-spec production: typically 2 to 4 weeks, plus ocean transit.
- Ocean transit varies by lane: roughly 2 weeks to East Asia, 4 to 6 weeks to the Middle East from a U.S. port.

## Standing programs
Recurring buyers move to a monthly supply plan with agreed cuts, grades, volume, and cadence, which shortens lead time and locks cut availability. Chilled programs in particular benefit from a fixed schedule because of shelf-life windows.
