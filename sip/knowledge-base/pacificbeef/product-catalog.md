# Pacific Beef Trading — Product Catalog

We supply the full carcass, organized by primal. Cuts are available bone-in or boneless, chilled or frozen, to buyer specification.

## Chuck (forequarter)
Chuck roll, chuck eye roll, chuck flap, blade, short ribs (chuck), clod. High-value in Korea and Japan for barbecue and hot-pot slicing.

## Rib
Ribeye roll (lip-on and lip-off), export rib, back ribs. Premium marbling grades in strong demand for chilled export.

## Loin
Striploin, tenderloin (PSMO), top sirloin butt, bottom sirloin (tri-tip, ball tip, flap meat). Chilled striploin and tenderloin are our highest-value export lines.

## Round (hindquarter)
Top round (inside), bottom round (outside, eye of round), knuckle, heel. Popular for lean, portion-cut, and further-processing markets.

## Brisket, plate, flank
Whole brisket (deckle-on/off), navel end / short plate, outside skirt, inside skirt, flank steak. Short plate is a core line for Korean and Japanese buyers.

## Variety meats and trimmings
Liver, heart, tongue, tripe, tendon, and assorted offal for markets that value them. Lean and fat trimmings (50/50 through 90/10) by chemical lean (CL) specification.

## Specifications
Every SKU ships against a written spec: cut, grade, weight range, packaging, chill/freeze state, and any trim or aging requirements. Custom cutting is available on standing orders above one container per month.
